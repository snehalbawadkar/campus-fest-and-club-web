Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about passes, teams, and campus entry.

Can I register for more than one event?

Yes. Select as many events as you like in the registration form below, provided their timings do not overlap.

Do I need a prior team for the hackathon or esports events?

You can register individually or with a team. We also host a brief team-matching mixer before kickoff.

Are students from other colleges welcome?

Yes, visiting college students with a valid student ID are welcome across competitions and evening shows.
